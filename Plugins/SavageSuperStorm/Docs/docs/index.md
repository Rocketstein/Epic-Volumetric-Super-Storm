# Savage Super Storm

A volumetric storm system for Unreal Engine, built on top of the engine's
Volumetric Cloud component. It renders a single large, art-directable storm —
mesocyclone body, anvil, spiral banding, underside detail — and gives artists
in-editor tools to author its shape, noise, motion, and vertical structure.

## What it gives you

**A storm actor you place and tune.** `Volumetric Super Storm Actor` carries the
storm's core, shape, noise, and motion settings, and drives the volumetric cloud
material for you. No manual material wiring on drop-in.

**Editor tools for authoring, not just sliders.** The plugin ships dedicated asset
editors — a tiling noise generator with 2D and volume preview, a wind flow-map
painter, and a vertical profile painter — so shape and motion are painted and
previewed rather than guessed at through numeric fields.

**Presets that travel.** Storm setups save to a preset asset and apply onto any
storm actor, with transitions for blending one profile into another.
